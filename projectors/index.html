Portable projector platforms for home theater and retail.

Our projector range covers smart mini projectors, portable LED models and home theater platforms. When comparing a projector, confirm native resolution, ANSI brightness, supported input, throw distance, operating system, keystone and connectivity rather than relying on headline claims alone.

What buyers should confirm

  • Native resolution versus supported input
  • ANSI brightness and projection size
  • Android, HDMI, USB and wireless options
  • Logo, packaging and target-market compliance
